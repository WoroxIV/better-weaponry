## 3.0.0

The combat system moved to its own mod, Combat Reforged, so that it can work with any weapon mod.

- With Combat Reforged installed, the weapons behave exactly as in 2.0. Your combat settings are imported automatically.
- Without it, the weapons keep their damage, speed, reach, recipes and enchantments, with damage rebalanced for a game without two-handed locks or pairs.
- Better Combat and Epic Fight now recognize every weapon, with their own reach and balance.
- Weapon reach turns off when another mod handles reach. The list is in `config/better_weaponry/compat.json`.
- Weapon reach now shows as a dark green stat under the attack speed, for example "5 Attack Reach". With Combat Reforged, Better Combat or Epic Fight, those mods show reach their own way.
- Vampirism, Execution, Bleeding and Returning stay here. Reflective, Riposte and Momentum moved to Combat Reforged.
