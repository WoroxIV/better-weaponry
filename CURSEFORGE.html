Better Weaponry

13 new weapon types, plus shields, bows, crossbows and shurikens, in every material.

Broadswords, spears, daggers, scythes, rapiers, sickles, katanas, battleaxes, glaives, hammers, claymores, chakrams and sais, from wood to netherite. Materials from many mods are supported too (Mekanism, Thermal, Create, Botania, Twilight Forest, and more).

Since 3.1.0: combat moved to Combat Ascended

Combos, parries, dashes, dual wielding and two-handed weapons are now in their own mod, Combat Ascended, so they work with any weapon mod.

  • Install Combat Ascended to keep the full combat system. With it, the weapons behave exactly as in 2.0, and your settings are imported automatically.
  • Without it, the weapons keep their damage, speed, reach, recipes and enchantments (Vampirism, Execution, Bleeding, Returning).
  • Better Combat and Epic Fight recognize every weapon.

Addons

Other mods can now add their own materials. Extended Weaponry adds copper, emerald, amethyst and Warden versions of every weapon, plus tools and armor.

Customization

Stats, reach and gear rules are JSON files in config/better_weaponry/, documented in a README written in your language. Data packs can change them too.

Available for NeoForge and Fabric 1.21.1, and Forge and Fabric 1.20.1.
